# Project Brief — BuildKart

## 1. Business & Brand Overview
- **Store Name:** BuildKart
- **Industry / Niche:** Construction & Building Materials
- **Core Products:** Cement, Bricks, Sand, and Steel (across multiple trusted brands)
- **Target Customers:** Contractors and Builders ordering for active construction sites
- **Brand Vibe & Personality:** Modern, fast, and easy to use — an online marketplace experience tailored for construction buyers

## 2. Catalog & Pricing
- **Launch Catalog Size:** 15 to 30 products across multiple brands
- **Price Range:** ₹500 to ₹1,00,000+ (covering individual bags/units up to bulk site orders)
- **Product Imagery:** Clean, realistic placeholder photos at launch (can be replaced with real warehouse/product photos anytime)

## 3. Website Pages & Structure
Full e-commerce storefront including:
1. **Home** — Hero section, featured categories (Cement, Bricks, Sand, Steel), popular brands, and quick access to order
2. **Shop** — Browse and filter all 15–30 products by category, brand, and price
3. **Product Detail** — Pricing, unit/quantity selector, brand details, and technical specifications
4. **Cart** — Review items, quantities, and estimated order total
5. **Checkout** — Delivery site address and payment method selection
6. **About** — BuildKart's story, reliability promise, and service standards for contractors
7. **Contact** — Quick WhatsApp support and business contact details

## 4. Visual Design & Branding
- **Color Palette:** Crisp White, Bright Safety Orange, and Charcoal
- **Typography:** Clean, modern, easy-to-read marketplace fonts
- **Design Inspiration:** Infra.Market-style industrial marketplace look and feel

## 5. Payments, Contact & Must-Have Features
- **Payment Methods:**
  - Payment Link (UPI / Cards)
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
- **Customer Support & Ordering:**
  - WhatsApp chat and quick-order button for fast on-site communication
- **Must-Have Feature:**
  - **Material Grade & Size Guide** (e.g., TMT steel bar mm sizes like 8mm/10mm/12mm/16mm, cement grades like OPC 43, OPC 53, PPC, brick/sand specs) to help contractors pick the right material fast

## 6. Domain Name
- **Status:** Not yet finalized
- **Suggested Option:** `buildkart.in` (can be updated to your preferred or existing domain)
